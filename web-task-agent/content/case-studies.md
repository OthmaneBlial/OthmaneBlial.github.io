# Case Studies

These three short studies show how to turn a web question into a reviewable decision. They use deterministic fixtures, not fresh research or adoption claims.

- [Decision Change Review](../case-studies/decision-change-review.md) — compare two packages and explain source, claim, and decision change.
- [Competitor Map](../case-studies/competitor-map.md) — keep positioning evidence, limitations, and a falsifiable gap together.
- [Launch Risk Review](../case-studies/launch-risk-review.md) — test whether a public promise survives a clean first-success path.

Each study names the command, decision, smallest next validation, and invalidation condition. Replace a fixture with a live run only after reviewing its source policy and model boundary.
