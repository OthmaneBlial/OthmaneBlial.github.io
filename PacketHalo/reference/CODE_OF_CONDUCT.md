# Code of conduct

PacketHalo contributors are expected to make participation welcoming, safe, and productive.

## Our standards

Examples of welcome behavior include respectful technical disagreement, actionable feedback, care with privacy-sensitive examples, and credit for other people's work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private network data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers, and community spaces where someone represents PacketHalo. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report urgent safety or privacy concerns through the repository's private GitHub reporting channel. For other conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through GitHub without publishing sensitive evidence. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as the available platform permits.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
