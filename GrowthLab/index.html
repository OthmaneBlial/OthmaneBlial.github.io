Make your idea
easier to test.

Start with a search or conversion question. GrowthLab keeps the idea, the page options and the checks together so the next step is clear.

  1. 01

    Start from a known page

    Bring the product page you want to improve and set the boundaries for the experiment.

    One starting point
  2. 02

    Explore three angles

    Try different headlines, sections, calls to action, or content ideas in separate safe copies.

    Three options
  3. 03

    Check what changed

    Open each page, see the changes, and run the checks that matter to your product.

    Clear evidence
  4. 04

    Choose your next test

    Pick the option you want to test, export it, or leave the original product exactly as it is.

    Your decision

Local by default

Your work stays on your machine.

The dashboard runs locally. The bundled demo needs no agent API key, and outside resources are blocked in previews.

Clear boundaries

You choose what may change.

Each experiment has an explicit goal and allowed area. The original product stays safe while options are being explored.

Honest evidence

The current demo records ideas and checks, not ranking or conversion results. Reports keep that distinction visible.
